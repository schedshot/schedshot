# SchedShot Support

Need help with SchedShot?

SchedShot converts screenshots of work schedules into calendar events on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

## Contact Support

If you're experiencing a problem with SchedShot, have a question, or would like to provide feedback, please contact:

**Email:** schedshot@icloud.com

When reporting a problem, please include:

- The device you're using (iPhone, iPad, or Mac)
- Your iOS, iPadOS, or macOS version
- A brief description of the problem
- Any relevant screenshots, if appropriate

Please do not include sensitive or private information in your support request.

## Privacy

SchedShot processes schedule screenshots on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Your schedule screenshots and extracted schedule information are not uploaded to SchedShot servers.

For more information, please see the SchedShot Privacy Policy.
