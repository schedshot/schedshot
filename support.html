Before You Send

A few details can make troubleshooting much faster.

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS, iPadOS, or macOS version
  • A short description of what happened
  • A screenshot if it helps explain the issue
⌗

Schedule Recognition

If SchedShot doesn't recognize a shift correctly, try using a clear screenshot with the dates, times, and schedule information visible.

◫

Calendar Access

SchedShot needs calendar permission to read existing events and create your work shifts.

You can review or change SchedShot's calendar access in your device's privacy settings.

●

Your schedule stays private.

SchedShot processes schedule screenshots directly on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Your screenshots and extracted schedule information are not uploaded to SchedShot servers.

No accounts. No tracking. No uploaded schedules. SchedShot is designed to keep schedule processing on your device.
