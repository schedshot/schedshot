Private by design

Your schedule
stays yours.

SchedShot is designed to perform its core functions directly on your device without collecting, storing, or transmitting your personal information to the developer.

Effective September 25, 2026 (Last Modified on September 30, 2026)

Information Collection

SchedShot does not collect, sell, or share personal information, and nothing you put into the app is sent to the developer.

The app has no servers of its own and does not connect to any. The only web pages it opens are this website's, and only when you tap a link to them.

The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics services.

No account. No tracking. No location. No uploaded schedules. Your schedule processing happens on your device.

Location

SchedShot does not ask for, collect, use, store, or transmit your location.

The app never requests Location permission and does not use GPS or any location services.

SchedShot never reads location information from your photos. Photos taken with a camera can carry the place they were taken as hidden information. SchedShot ignores it.

The only hidden detail SchedShot reads from an image, besides the text you can see on it, is the date and time the image was created.

It uses that to work out the year of a schedule that doesn't print one. That detail is not saved, and nothing is sent anywhere.

About the “Location” option in Apple's photo picker. When you choose photos, iOS shows Apple's own photo picker.

Depending on your version of iOS, it may include an Options menu with a Location switch, which lets you decide whether a photo's location is shared with an app.

That picker and its switches are built and controlled by Apple, not by SchedShot.

Whether the switch is on or off, SchedShot does not use location. Screenshots normally don't contain any location information at all.

About the Location field on your shifts. When a schedule lists where a shift is, such as a store name, SchedShot copies that text into the calendar event's Location field. It comes from the words on your screenshot, not from where you or your device are.

Screenshot and Image Processing

SchedShot allows you to provide screenshots or images containing schedule information, by choosing them in the app, sharing them to SchedShot, or, on a Mac, choosing or dragging in image files.

Image and text recognition is performed locally on your device using Apple's Vision framework.

Images and information extracted from them are not uploaded to SchedShot servers or transmitted to the developer.

SchedShot does not keep your screenshots. Once the shifts are read, the image is not saved by the app, and SchedShot does not maintain a remote copy of your screenshots or extracted schedule information.

Calendar Access

SchedShot requests full access to your device's calendar so that it can read calendar information and create and manage calendar events based on the screenshot information you provide.

Calendar access may be used to:

  • Create calendar events from recognized date and time information in screenshots.
  • Read existing calendar events as necessary for scheduling functionality, such as showing your upcoming shifts.
  • Help prevent duplicate or conflicting schedule entries, and notice when a new schedule changes or drops a shift you already added.
  • Change, rename, or delete shifts SchedShot added, when you ask it to (for example, editing a shift, applying a time change, or renaming a job).
  • Create the calendar you choose for your shifts, if it doesn't exist yet.

Calendar information is processed on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or to SchedShot servers.

You can change or revoke SchedShot's calendar permissions at any time through your device's privacy settings.

Widgets

Widgets show your upcoming shifts on your Home Screen or Lock Screen (or your Mac's desktop); they read your calendar on your device and send nothing anywhere.

A Lock Screen widget can be seen without unlocking your device. You choose whether to add one.

Photos and Images

When you choose an image or screenshot for processing, SchedShot accesses only the content made available to the app through Apple's system interfaces and permissions.

The selected content is used to identify schedule information and provide the app's requested functionality.

SchedShot does not upload these images to the developer.

SchedShot does not read or use the location information that may be stored in a photo. See Location above.

Settings and iCloud Sync

SchedShot saves its settings on your device, such as your chosen calendar, default event title, alert time, appearance, and header picture.

The job names and colors you set, and colors you give to single shifts, are also kept in sync between your own devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, under your iCloud account. The developer cannot see this information.

If iCloud is turned off for SchedShot or you are not signed in to iCloud, these stay on the device only.

Data Sharing

Because SchedShot does not collect personal data, the developer does not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Tracking and Advertising

SchedShot does not track users across apps or websites.

SchedShot does not contain third-party advertising and does not use user information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Children's Privacy

SchedShot does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the app does not collect personal information from its users, no personal information about children is knowingly transmitted to or retained by the developer.

Data Security

SchedShot is designed to minimize privacy risks by processing information and images locally on your device rather than transmitting them to the developer.

Your information may still be subject to the security and privacy practices of Apple and services you have configured on your device, such as iCloud or Google Calendar. Events SchedShot adds to those calendars are synced by those services.

This Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, or advertising. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

Your light or dark appearance choice for this website is saved only in your browser.
