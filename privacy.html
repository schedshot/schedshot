Private by design

Your schedule
stays yours.

SchedShot is designed to perform its core functions directly on your device without collecting, storing, or transmitting your personal information to the developer.

Effective September 25, 2026

Information Collection

SchedShot does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics services.

No account. No tracking. No uploaded schedules. Your schedule processing happens on your device.

Screenshot and Image Processing

SchedShot allows you to provide screenshots or images containing work schedule information.

Image and text recognition is performed locally on your device using Apple's Vision framework.

Images and information extracted from them are not uploaded to SchedShot servers or transmitted to the developer.

SchedShot does not maintain a remote copy of your screenshots or extracted schedule information.

Calendar Access

SchedShot requests access to your device's calendar so that it can read calendar information and create calendar events based on the schedules you provide.

Calendar access may be used to:

  • Create calendar events from recognized work shifts.
  • Read existing calendar events as necessary for scheduling functionality.
  • Help prevent duplicate or conflicting schedule entries.

Calendar information is processed on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or to SchedShot servers.

You can change or revoke SchedShot's calendar permissions at any time through your device's privacy settings.

Photos and Images

When you choose an image or screenshot for processing, SchedShot accesses only the content made available to the app through Apple's system interfaces and permissions.

The selected content is used to identify schedule information and provide the app's requested functionality.

SchedShot does not upload these images to the developer.

Data Sharing

Because SchedShot does not collect personal data, the developer does not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Tracking and Advertising

SchedShot does not track users across apps or websites.

SchedShot does not contain third-party advertising and does not use user information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Children's Privacy

SchedShot does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the app does not collect personal information from its users, no personal information about children is knowingly transmitted to or retained by the developer.

Data Security

SchedShot is designed to minimize privacy risks by processing schedule information and images locally on your device rather than transmitting them to the developer.

Your information may still be subject to the security and privacy practices of Apple and services you have configured on your device, such as iCloud Calendar.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if SchedShot's features or data practices change.

Any updated policy will include a revised effective date.
