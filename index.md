# SchedShot

**Turn a screenshot of your work schedule into calendar events.**

SchedShot makes adding your work schedule to your calendar quick and easy. 
Choose a screenshot, review the detected shifts, and add them directly to 
your calendar.

## Privacy First

Schedule recognition happens directly on your device using Apple's Vision 
framework. Your screenshots aren't uploaded to SchedShot servers.
